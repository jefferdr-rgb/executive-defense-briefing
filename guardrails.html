Operational Integrity & Risk Controls

Enterprise Guardrails, Stop-Lines & The Pilot

Answering the tough questions: strict data isolation, where automation stops, stakeholder sign-offs, and pilot guarantees.

The #1 GovCon Concern: "Could Client A's Rates or Win Themes Ever Leak to Client B?"

Absolute Zero Data Contamination. In defense contracting, wrap rates, labor pricing, and win themes are trade secrets. This software is not a shared multi-tenant cloud service. Each defense contractor receives their own dedicated, locally deployed appliance. Customer proposal text and pricing data are never used to train any shared AI model. Client A's data is cryptographically and physically impossible for Client B to ever see.

The Stop-Lines: What The Machine Does vs. What It Refuses To Do

Autonomy that claims to do everything is untrustworthy. In GovCon, a proposal requires human legal accountability. Here is the exact boundary where software automation ends and human executive authority begins:

Workflow Step Execution Mode Operational Boundary
RFP Shredding & Compliance Matrix Automated Extracts 100% of "shall", "will", and Section L/M requirements into an audit spreadsheet in 5 minutes.
Past Performance & Resume Mapping Automated Indexes Deltek/Costpoint records and corporate past performance repos to find exact qualification matches.
Initial Volume Drafting Automated Generates 100% compliant draft text with cross-references to solicitation sections and requirements.
Pricing Strategy & Wrap Rates HUMAN ONLY The system never sets pricing strategy. A human Pricing Director inputs approved rates. The software only performs penny-arithmetic verification.
Customer Relationship & Teaming HUMAN ONLY The system does not shape procurements. Customer discovery, agency calls, teaming partner agreements, and Black Hat reviews remain 100% human.
Final Proposal Submission HUMAN ONLY The system cannot submit proposals. Final upload and formal submission to SAM.gov, PIEE, or DoD portals requires explicit human signature and sign-off.
