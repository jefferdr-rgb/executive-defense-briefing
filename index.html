Defense Proposal Software

A simple overview of what was built, how it works, and the core business decision.

What This Is In One Sentence

We built software that runs on an office server to automatically read government solicitations, check every requirement, pull company past performance, and write audit-ready defense proposals in Microsoft Word in 4 hours instead of 3 weeks.

1. Why Defense Contractors Need This
6–8 Bids

The Human Limit

A good proposal manager caps out at 6 to 8 bids a year. Hiring more senior capture people is slow, expensive ($250k+ each), and eats profit margins.

0 Errors

Disqualification Trap

The government throws out proposals for minor errors—like missing one small requirement or having numbers in the pricing sheet that don't match the written proposal.

100% Local

Can't Use Cloud AI

Defense contractors cannot paste sensitive rates, resumes, or contract data into ChatGPT or cloud tools without violating federal cybersecurity rules.

2. How The Software Works
01

Reads the Solicitation

Scans government RFP documents (Sections C, L, and M) and extracts every single mandatory requirement in 60 seconds.

02

Locks the Checklist

Builds a 100% complete compliance matrix. Writing cannot start until every government requirement has an assigned section in the proposal outline.

03

Matches Past Performance

Searches the company's past contracts, resumes, and Deltek labor categories to match real qualifications to the bid.

04

Drafts & Audits

Drafts compliant technical sections, checks for weak language, and verifies that written staffing matches the pricing spreadsheet to the penny.

05

Builds the Word File (.docx)

Creates a properly formatted Microsoft Word document with tables, headers, and required government distribution markings ready for executive review.
