SECTION 01 OVERVIEW

Autonomous Proposal Platform

An on-premise, secure software platform that turns multi-week defense proposal efforts into automated, audit-ready submissions in hours.

The Purpose of This Review
This briefing summarizes a defense proposal platform built in pure Go that runs entirely on local servers inside a defense contractor's facility. It automates the most time-consuming parts of proposal capture—extracting solicitation requirements, drafting compliant technical narrative, and matching labor pricing to narrative commitments—with zero cloud data transmission and 100% auditable mathematical accuracy.
01 The Core Solution
An automated 6-stage proposal engine that ingests government solicitations (RFP Sections C, L, and M), builds a complete compliance matrix, pulls from company past performance, and produces formatted Microsoft Word (.docx) documents.
02 Why It Is Different
Unlike public AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) that hallucinate facts and violate federal security rules, this platform runs 100% on-premise with built-in mathematical checks that prevent dropped requirements and pricing errors.

CAPABILITY 01 COMPLIANCE

100% Requirement Coverage Lock

Preventing proposal disqualification by mathematically tracking every government requirement.

The Compliance Guarantee
In traditional capture, writers frequently overlook minor clauses buried in contract attachments (e.g., specific security training or reporting standards). The government evaluator finds a blank score sheet and issues a deficiency rating. Our system decomposes all "shall" and "must" statements into a database and locks drafting until 100% are mapped to the outline.
Automated Clause Extraction
Parses federal solicitations and extracts every mandatory requirement with its specific reference number (e.g., Section C.3.4.1).
Order of Precedence Check
Identifies contradictions between Section L (Instructions) and Section M (Evaluation) per FAR 52.215-8, preventing compliance confusion.
Amendment Tracking
When the government issues solicitation amendments or Q&A changes, the system highlights exact text changes and flags affected sections.
CAPABILITY 02 FINANCIAL INTEGRITY

Cost vs. Technical Volume Reconciliation

Eliminating discrepancies between pricing spreadsheets and technical volume narrative.

The Common Cost Disconnect
A proposal narrative states: "We will dedicate 4 Senior Cyber Specialists on-site." But the Cost Volume pricing model only budgets for 3 FTEs. The contracting officer catches the mismatch and issues a cost realism penalty or disqualifies the proposal.
Automated Number Extraction
The software scans technical narrative for headcount, labor categories, hours, and software licenses, extracting all quantitative claims.
Exact Dollar Verification
Compares extracted numbers directly against Deltek Costpoint spreadsheets. If there is a single dollar or fraction of an FTE difference, the platform flags the error immediately.
DCAA Audit Readiness
Produces a complete audit log proving that narrative commitments align with certified cost and pricing data.
CAPABILITY 03 REVIEW AUDIT

Automated Compliance & Quality Audit

Simulating federal Source Selection Evaluation Board (SSEB) reviews before human Red Teams meet.

Unsparing Internal Audit
Human review teams are often polite, exhausted, and influenced by internal politics. The platform runs an automated audit seeded with official federal evaluation standards (Strengths, Weaknesses, Significant Weaknesses, Deficiencies) to catch weak narrative before customers see it.
Rating Standard Government SSEB Definition Automated Platform Check
Blue (Outstanding) Multiple significant strengths, zero weaknesses. Audits for verified discriminators, metrics, and past performance citations.
Purple (Good) Strengths outweigh weaknesses, low risk. Confirms complete clause coverage with clear proof points.
Green (Acceptable) Meets minimum requirements, acceptable risk. Baseline requirement fulfillment.
Red (Unacceptable) One or more Deficiencies present. HARD STOP: Flags any missing mandatory clause for immediate revision.
CAPABILITY 04 OUTPUT QUALITY

Native Microsoft Word (.docx) Generation

Generating properly formatted Word documents with compliant tables, callouts, and headers.

Solving the Formatting Breakdown
Most AI tools output simple Markdown text. When converting Markdown into Microsoft Word, complex multi-column compliance tables, callout boxes, headers, footers, and page margins collapse. Defense proposals must be submitted in Microsoft Word or PDF. Our platform compiles directly into native Word OpenXML (.docx) format with zero conversion flaws.
Compliant Tables
Generates multi-column compliance matrices with shaded headers, bold lead-ins, and exact column widths.
CUI Banners & Headers
Automatically injects mandatory DoD Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) running headers, footers, and distribution markings.
Page Budget Enforcement
Tracks word counts per section to ensure narrative fits strictly within government page limits.

Key Questions for Strategic Review

The core business and positioning questions to discuss with your advisor.

Question 1 Single Contractor Exclusivity vs. Market Licensing

"If a contractor offers equity to lock this platform in exclusively, what is that equity realistically worth given defense services trade at 1.0x–1.5x revenue? Does it make more sense to keep the software IP in an independent LLC and license it, or surrender ownership for employee equity?"

Question 2 Market Appetite & Pricing Acceptance

"Would mid-tier defense contractors pay $40k for installation plus $7k/month for an on-premise, secure proposal system that guarantees zero compliance drift? Or is the market still hesitant to adopt AI in capture operations?"

Question 3 Organizational Conflict of Interest (OCI)

"If I serve as an executive at one defense contractor, how quickly do we trigger FAR 9.505 OCI issues if the software is used across bids where that company competes against other clients?"

Question 4 Preferred Positioning for Partnership

"What is the cleanest structure to partner with an anchor contractor: grant them preferred licensing rights and joint win upside as the flagship proof point, while retaining 100% ownership of the underlying software?"

Recommended Path
Retain 100% ownership of the software platform in an independent entity. Offer the primary contractor preferred anchor pricing and customized past performance integration to serve as the validated flagship deployment, while preserving your ability to license across the broader defense market.
